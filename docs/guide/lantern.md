# 🏮 Lantern

Lantern is the part of Gravecast you keep on your phone. Open [grave-cast.vercel.app/lantern](https://grave-cast.vercel.app/lantern/) and add it to your home screen. It then opens like an app, even in flight mode.

**Install it before you need it.** In an emergency, there may be no internet to download it.

Lantern is plain and fast on purpose: no 3D, no effects, no decorative sound. Only sounds that do a job play, like the CPR beat and SOS.

## What it holds

| | What it does | Needs internet? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Survival kit** | 31 first aid and survival guides, from CPR, choking and burns to floods, earthquakes and safe water. Each is a stick-figure comic with every step also written out, and each names its source. Any guide can be read aloud. | No |
| **Tools** | SOS light and SOS sound in Morse, a CPR beat, a compass, a lightning distance timer, and a help message with your position, ready to send by text. | No |
| **Chat** | Write a message any time. It waits on your phone and goes out by itself when the signal comes back. | A little |
| **Radio** | A pretend shortwave radio with nine channels. Messages are read aloud, or played as old radio tones. | A little |
| **Access** | A braille pad for people who can't see or speak, a pad of ready messages for people who can't hear or speak, and a distress signal that can sound, vibrate silently, and text a help contact. | No |
| **Help near you** | Hospitals, pharmacies, police and fire stations nearby. The list is saved on your phone, so it still works offline. | Once, to find them |

## Good to know

- **The guides are a prototype.** They come from recognised sources (IFRC, WHO, AHA) but haven't been medically reviewed yet. Always follow official guidance and your local emergency services.
- **Chat and Radio are public.** Don't post personal details.
- **Your position stays with you.** It's saved only in your browser. Help near you sends it straight from your phone to the map service to find places, and Gravecast's own server never sees it.
- **Nothing contacts anyone by itself.** The help message and distress signal open your text app, and you still tap Send.
